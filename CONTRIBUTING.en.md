# Contributing

Contributions of adaptations for different factory firmware versions, hardware assembly photos, calibration methods, and reproduction results are welcome.

Describe the printer and board, actual Klipper and Moonraker versions, service interpreters, driver model/resistor/jumpers, motor sides viewed from the front, and which features were physically validated versus checked offline. Explain the concrete problem the change solves and a repeatable validation method.

Before sharing, remove actual IP addresses, access keys, personal paths, job filenames, private models, network settings, and sensitive information in raw logs. Public factory credentials mks/makerbase may illustrate default login, but do not submit someone else's changed password.

Retain third-party copyrights and GPL notices. For unfamiliar factory versions, make the minimum interface adaptation needed; do not submit wholesale upgrades, full configuration replacement, or disabled safeguards as an error workaround. Keep mechanical parameters and first-layer corrections identified as machine-specific data. Success on one printer is not proof of compatibility across the whole product line.

The repository does not promise support response times or certify batch compatibility. Provide the necessary sanitized information in an issue rather than uploading the printer's entire filesystem.
